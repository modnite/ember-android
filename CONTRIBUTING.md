# Contribution and release notes

Describe changes in terms of product behavior, reproduction steps and validation. Keep known limitations explicit.

Do not include personal names, private conversation excerpts, identifying user/device details or commentary about a conversation in release notes, source comments, examples or screenshots. Use synthetic fixture data. Retain legitimate authorship and licence attribution.

Before publishing, review the diff and release text for accidental personal information. Record enough technical detail to reproduce a bug without identifying the person who reported it.
